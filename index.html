Thermomix Quisotto with Mushrooms, Spinach & Buffalo Feta

Serves: 2 generous portions

Ingredients

  • 120 g quinoa, well rinsed
  • 150 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 carrot, cut into chunks
  • 1 handful spinach
  • 350–400 g chicken broth
  • 15 g olive oil
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp sweet paprika
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1–2 tsp lemon juice
  • Fresh chives or parsley
  • 100–150 g cooked chicken, shredded
  • 60–80 g buffalo feta-style cheese, crumbled

Preparation

  1. Chop carrot: Add carrot to mixing bowl. 4 sec / speed 5. Scrape down sides.
  2. Sauté: Add 15 g olive oil and 150 g sliced mushrooms. 7 min / 120°C / reverse / speed spoon. Leave measuring cup off.
  3. Toast quinoa: Add rinsed quinoa, cumin, paprika and black pepper. 2 min / 120°C / reverse / speed spoon.
  4. Cook: Add 350 g chicken broth. 17 min / 98°C / reverse / speed spoon. Place simmering basket on lid instead of measuring cup. If needed, cook another 2–3 min / 98°C / reverse / speed spoon.
  5. Add spinach and chicken: Add spinach and shredded cooked chicken. 2 min / 90°C / reverse / speed spoon.
  6. Finish: Transfer to a bowl. Stir through lemon juice and, if desired, a drizzle of olive oil. Taste before adding salt.
  7. Serve: Divide into two portions and crumble 30–40 g buffalo feta-style cheese over each. Finish with chives or parsley and black pepper.

For next-day lunch

Keep feta separate if possible. Reheat the quisotto with 1–2 tbsp water or chicken broth, then add the feta afterwards.
